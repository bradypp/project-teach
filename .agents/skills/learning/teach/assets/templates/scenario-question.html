The missing receipt

A webhook worker asks an email service to send a message. The worker crashes before storing a local completion receipt.

The event is still pending locally. The email service may have accepted the request, but the worker did not retain its response.
What can the restarted worker conclude from the pending event alone?

A local pending marker can coexist with either remote outcome. The missing receipt is an information gap, not proof of failure or success.

Choose the next move and explain what evidence or contract it needs.
A hint

Separate the question “did it happen?” from “can repeating it be safe?”

Compare possible approaches
  • A durable remote receipt could settle the outcome if the service lets you look it up reliably.
  • A stable operation key can make retry safe when the service guarantees deduplication for that key and retry window.
  • Without either contract, describe the duplicate-send risk and how a human would resolve it.
