Switchboard: the investigation contract

Created 16th Sept 2026 · 21:44

A compact implementation aid for the first read-only investigation path. These are proposed project decisions, ready to compare against code and traces.

At the API boundary

FieldInvariant
tenant_idDerived from verified identity; applied to jobs and evidence access
request_keyUnique with tenant_id; a retry resolves to the same job
deadlineAbsolute job deadline; never reset by a retry
claim_generationRequired for ownership-sensitive writes
statusQueued, running, completed, failed, cancelled or expired

Inside the evidence packet

  • Source ID, section, version and tenant.
  • Relevant excerpt and retrieval provenance.
  • Incident time and source freshness where they affect interpretation.
  • Actual packed token cost, plus reserved output allowance.

Keep the full candidate list in the trace and the selected packet in graph state. A source ID without its version is insufficient for reproducing an answer after a document edit.

At completion

  • Persist the answer and citations, or a clear abstention/partial-result reason.
  • Reject writes from a superseded claim generation.
  • Record time, tool calls, token usage and relevant policy versions.
  • Keep “job completed” separate from “answer supported” in evaluation.
